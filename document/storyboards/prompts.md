# Prompt set — storyboard v1

Mode: built-in image generation. No CLI/API fallback. The Nemo and Corgi prompts below preserve the generation brief; the Mèo Cam prompt is the exact final prompt used. Images are concept review sheets, not production sprites.

## Shared brief — Nemo and Corgi

Use case: illustration-story. Animation storyboard for cheerful hydration mobile game, review concept only. Portrait 12 panels, 4 rows by 3 columns. Locked medium-wide camera, consistent identity. Soft polished 2.5D storybook illustration, cream paper, turquoise water, pastel island grass, gentle sunlight. Full body gestures, anticipation, squash/stretch, follow-through, dotted movement arcs. Header character name. Columns START, PEAK, SETTLE. Rows IDLE 5s, TAP 2s, DRINK 3s, GOAL 6s. Each panel one pose. Short clean labels, no paragraphs, app UI or watermark.

## Nemo

Header NEMO. Lovable recognizable orange Nemo clownfish, three white bands edged black, one small fin, expressive eyes. Underwater lagoon, coral arch, sea anemone. IDLE peeks from anemone, follows rising bubble, settles waving fins. TAP notices surface ripple, darts up leaving bubbles, noses bubble that pops. DRINK notices food pellets, curls upward and nibbles, happy tight loop. GOAL anticipates above coral, looping figure eight with bubbles and small reef fish, settles proudly among heart bubbles. Fish always in water.

## Corgi

Header CORGI. Pembroke Welsh Corgi, fawn orange and white, big upright ears, four short legs, compact long body, fluffy rump. Sunny grassy island, toy ball, dog house. IDLE sleeps chin on paws, stretches front paws rump high, sits tilted head. TAP spots rolling ball, enthusiastic run ears bouncing, catches ball proudly. DRINK perks ears at food bowl, runs and comic skid then eats, sits licking lips and happy rump wiggle. GOAL crouches, runs circular lap with teal scarf and tiny hop, proud seat among leaf confetti. Correct canine anatomy.
