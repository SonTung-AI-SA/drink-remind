# Prompt brief — pet assets v1

Mode: built-in image generation, transparent_background=true. Reference: corresponding approved document/storyboards/*-storyboard-v1.png. This records the generation brief.

## Shared

Use case: stylized-concept. Transparent PNG character key-pose atlas for a mobile pet game. Approved storyboard is character identity and pose reference only. Exactly 12 isolated full-body poses, strict uniform 3-column x 4-row grid. Even spacing, same proportions and scale, 15% cell padding. Soft polished 2.5D storybook rendering, matching reference colors, faces and markings. Rows IDLE, TAP, DRINK reward, GOAL. Three poses per row: beginning, peak, settle. No scenery, paper, borders, labels, arrows, shadows or watermark. Props only when needed and contained within each cell. Preserve true transparency.

## Nemo

Orange clownfish, three white bands edged black, one consistently small pectoral fin. Idle: relaxed hover, curious upward look, smiling hover. Tap: looks up, upward dart, nose touches bubble with eyes closed. Drink: sees food pellet, nibbles pellet, joyful curved swimming pose. Goal: anticipates turn, graceful banking loop, proud forward smile with small heart bubbles. No coral, other fish or drawn water.

## Corgi

Orange fawn and white Corgi, large ears, four short legs, long compact body, fluffy rump. Idle: asleep chin on paws, front paw stretch rump up, seated tilted head. Tap: alert to red ball, airborne playful run, holds ball. Drink: sees blue bowl visibly containing brown kibble rather than water, bends eating, sits licking lips next to empty bowl. Goal: crouches with teal scarf, joyful running hop scarf trailing, proud seated smile with scarf.
